Hey everyone! I built **ClaudeMeter Pro** — a macOS menu bar app that tracks your Claude.ai usage in real time.

**Why:** I kept getting throttled without realizing I was near the limit. The usage info on claude.ai is buried, so I wanted something always visible.

**What it does:**
- Live session/daily/weekly usage % with countdown in your menu bar
- 9 display styles (ring, pill, dot, battery, gauge, etc.)
- Desktop widgets (macOS 14+) — always visible on your desktop
- Usage history charts (5h to 90d) with activity heatmaps
- Smart notifications at 75%, 90%, 100%
- Predictions based on your consumption rate
- All data stored locally — no server, no telemetry
